## 2. A nucleophile is best described as…

* A) An electron-pair donor
* B) An electron-pair acceptor
* C) A proton source
* D) A spectral peak

Correct Answer: A) An electron-pair donor
------------------------------
## 3. SN2 reactions are characterized by…

* A) Backside attack and inversion
* B) A carbocation intermediate
* C) Two-step elimination
* D) Aromatic resonance only

Correct Answer: A) Backside attack and inversion
------------------------------
## 4. Enantiomers are molecules that are…

* A) Non-superimposable mirror images
* B) Identical constitutional isomers
* C) Always achiral
* D) Different conformers only

Correct Answer: A) Non-superimposable mirror images
------------------------------
## 5. IR spectroscopy is especially useful for identifying…

* A) Functional-group vibrations
* B) Molecular mass only
* C) Reaction yield
* D) Optical rotation alone

Correct Answer: A) Functional-group vibrations
------------------------------
## 6. In a resonance hybrid, the real molecule has…
